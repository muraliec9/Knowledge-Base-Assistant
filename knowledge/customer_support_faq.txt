# Customer Support FAQ

## Account

### How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings > Security > Change Password. Enter your current password and then your new password twice. If you no longer have access to your registered email address, contact Customer Support.

### Can I change my email address?
Yes. Go to Settings > Account > Email Address. You will need to verify the new email address before the change takes effect.

### How long does account deletion take?
Account deletion is normally completed within 7 business days. Some records may be retained longer where required by law or for fraud prevention.

## Billing

### When am I charged?
Monthly subscriptions are charged on the same calendar day that the subscription started. If that date does not exist in a particular month, the charge occurs on the last day of that month.

### Can I get a refund?
Annual subscription refunds are available within 14 days of the original purchase if the account has not exceeded 10 GB of data usage. Monthly subscriptions are generally non-refundable.

### What happens if my payment fails?
We retry the payment three times over a period of 7 days. During this period, your account remains active. If all retries fail, the account is placed into read-only mode.

## Support

### What are the support hours?
Standard support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM UTC.

### How quickly will support respond?
Standard support tickets receive an initial response within one business day. Enterprise customers with Priority Support receive an initial response within four business hours.
