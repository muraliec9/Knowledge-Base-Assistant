# Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-REMOTE-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: Full-time and part-time employees

## 1. Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees in their first 30 days of employment are not eligible for regular remote work unless an exception is approved by Human Resources.

## 2. Core Hours

All employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time on their scheduled working days.

Employees may arrange the remaining working hours flexibly with their manager.

## 3. Equipment

The company provides one laptop and one external monitor to eligible employees.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection.

Additional equipment requires manager approval and may be subject to budget limitations.

## 4. Remote Work Location

Employees must work from their registered home address unless they receive written approval for another location.

Working from another country requires approval from both the employee's manager and Human Resources.

## 5. Security

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from public networks.

Company devices must use full-disk encryption.

Employees must not allow family members or other unauthorized individuals to use company devices.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted for medical, accessibility, or business reasons.

All exceptions must be documented through Human Resources.

## 7. Violations

Repeated violations may result in suspension of remote-work privileges.

Serious security violations may result in disciplinary action under the company's information-security policy.
