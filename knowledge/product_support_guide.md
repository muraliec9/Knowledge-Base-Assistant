# Product support guide

## Creating a support request
Search the known-issues page before opening a request. A useful request includes the product version, environment, clear steps to reproduce, expected behaviour, actual behaviour, and any non-sensitive logs. The support team confirms receipt within one business day.

## Severity levels
Use Severity 1 only for a complete outage or a critical safety or security impact. Severity 2 is a major impairment with no practical workaround. Severity 3 covers standard issues with a workaround available. Support may adjust severity based on impact and urgency.
