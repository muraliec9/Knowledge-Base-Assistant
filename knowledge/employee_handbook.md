# Employee handbook: everyday support

## Working hours and flexible work
Employees should agree their normal working pattern with their line manager. Flexible working is supported when it meets team and business needs. Submit requests through the people portal and discuss the request with your manager before changing your regular schedule.

## IT support
For access issues, begin with the service portal and include the application name, error message, device type, and a screenshot if appropriate. Urgent incidents that prevent a business-critical activity should be marked as high priority. Never include passwords or sensitive patient information in a ticket.

## New starter checklist
In your first week, complete mandatory security training, confirm access to essential systems, and schedule a welcome meeting with your manager. Your manager is responsible for assigning a buddy and reviewing role-specific learning.
