# Compliance FAQ

## Reporting a concern
Anyone who becomes aware of a suspected compliance concern should report it promptly through their manager, the compliance contact, or the confidential Speak Up channel. Reports made in good faith are treated seriously and retaliation is not tolerated. Preserve relevant information, but do not conduct your own investigation.

## Handling confidential information
Use approved company systems and share confidential information only with people who have a legitimate business need. Check the data classification before sharing files externally. If you believe information was sent to the wrong recipient, contact Information Security immediately and notify your manager.

## Gifts and invitations
Before accepting or offering a gift, meal, or invitation, review the applicable policy and any local limits. When in doubt, seek guidance from Compliance before proceeding.
